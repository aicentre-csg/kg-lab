About us

Making connected knowledge useful

We are a research lab in the AI Centre at City St George's, University of London. Our work brings together knowledge representation, semantic technologies, machine learning and data integration to build trustworthy, interoperable knowledge-driven systems.

We welcome collaborations with researchers, public-sector organisations and industry partners.

Research

Research lines

01

Ontology Alignment

Scalable ontology alignment and alignment repair. Leveraging LLM as oracles.

Tools: LogMap, LogMapLLM

02

Knowledge Graph Validation

Scalable logical reasoning for knowledge graph validation and quality assurance.

Tools: KG validation

03

Ontology Embeddings

Learning embeddings that capture ontology semantics for downstream machine learning applications.

Tools: OWL2Vec*, ELK-Em

04

Neuro-symbolic AI

Combining OWL-based KGs with learning methods for transparent and robust AI.

Tools: KGR4VRD, NeSy4VRD
