Introduction

Knowledge that works together

The Knowledge Graphs Lab is part of the AI Centre at City St George's, University of London.

Our research brings together knowledge representation, semantic technologies, machine learning and data integration. We are particularly interested in robust methods that make AI systems more interoperable, transparent and trustworthy.

We welcome collaborations with academic, public-sector and industry partners.

Research lines

Knowledge graph construction

02

Ontology alignment & integration

Connecting independently developed data models and vocabularies to enable semantic interoperability.

03

Reasoning & validation

Scalable logical reasoning, constraint checking and quality assurance for dependable knowledge-based applications.

04

Neuro-symbolic & trustworthy AI

Combining structured knowledge and machine learning to create robust, transparent and explainable AI systems.
